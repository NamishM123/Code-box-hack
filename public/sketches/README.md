# Product page plates

The product page renders these two files, bordered, through EditorialPage's
gallery:

  room-study-01.svg   room corner, ink and wash
  room-study-02.svg   seating group, line

Both are drawn in-repo as stand-ins. To use your own artwork, drop it in
under the same names, or keep your extension and update the two `src`
values in `src/app/product/page.tsx`.
